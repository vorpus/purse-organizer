# Rimowa insert requirements

## Established fit and print setup

The user tested the 180 × 100 × 3 mm V1 frame. Its footprint fits well.
It stops where the lined sides taper inward, about 5 mm ahead of the back.
Usable depth is approximately 20 mm. Printer is an Ender 3 V3 SE with a
0.4 mm nozzle and PLA.

## Construction

The frame and organizer form one connected printable object. Preserve separate
named FRAME and ORGANIZER source collections and separate geometry functions
so future layouts can reuse the fitted frame. The user clarified that mechanical
separation into removable modules is unnecessary.

Use solid side walls for this prototype to simplify printing, with an open backing.
Minimize accessible support material and avoid trapped support.

## Item arrangement

- Anker MagGo 622 sideways at lower left, measured approximately 105 × 67 × 13 mm.
- Touchland upright at right, user measured 55 × 95 × 17 mm.
- To Summer solid perfume horizontal above the battery, with its broad face
  parallel to the organizer back, approximately
  66.4 × 25.3 × 16.2 mm from the aligned scan, confirmed approximately by user.
- Perfume should sit in a smooth cradle, resist movement in the upright bag, and
  lift upward to release. Battery can tilt forward and then lift out.
- The perfume case should use regular geometry informed by the reference photos.

## Fit iteration

The user reported a good fit from the shared-base V2 print and authorized the
production version. Carry its 2 mm rear inset and 5 mm taper length into the
production frame. Verify the revised pockets and removal paths physically
after printing the production model.

Battery and perfume share the frame backing. Battery rests on the frame bottom;
perfume rests in two elevated curved end cradles.
Leave the battery's upper edge open, without a surrounding top wall, to make
removal easier. The perfume retains only its own two end supports.

## Production refinement

Build smooth integrated pockets based on the approved visual concept, with
solid sides, deliberate openings in the backing, and rounded contact edges.
Use two curved pill-like end cradles for the perfume, with a 15 mm central gap
(user requested 10-20 mm). The central thumb gap
must remove the bottom shelf as well as the front retainer, leaving no bridge
under the middle of the perfume. Connect the two cradles through the frame
and backing so this remains one printable object. Preserve the horizontal perfume
orientation and its broad face parallel to the organizer back.

Avoid stacked plates, thick junction fills, and redundant inner side walls.
Use thin flowing shells, a shorter center divider, and a much lower Touchland
front lip. Preserve the fitted outside contour while reducing unnecessary
material on both outer sides.
The Touchland lip must flow continuously into its side walls as one curved
pocket. Perfume support sections must join the backing smoothly without a
reverse-curled bottom or separate-looking ledge.
Remove the mirrored rear panels behind the perfume: the purse lining supports
its back. Keep only the structural connections needed for one printable body.
Blend each pocket's side walls into its lower lip so the opening itself has
one continuous silhouette, without steps or overlapping outlines.
